
## Overview 

**Scene Type**: Introduction

**Setting**: Wilderness

**Encounter Type**: Roleplaying, Investigative

**Opening Description:** You enter a clearing where sunlight is weak and strange.

A man kneels near a dead tree.

Not weeping.

Not praying.

Just touching the vines growing through it.

He does not turn immediately.

Then he says:

> “You came out alive.”

## Actions

The Druid's grip tightens on his staff.

For the first time, his calm expression slips — his jaw clenches, his breathing catches, and his shoulders tense as if he is holding something inside by force alone.

A sharp crack cuts through the clearing.

His body folds forward suddenly, one hand driving into the earth while the other still grips the staff. Another crack follows — louder this time — ribs shifting beneath his clothes, shoulders widening in violent jerks.

Dark fur pushes through fabric along his arms and neck.

His fingers lengthen around the wood, nails blackening into claws, yet somehow he does not let go.

The roots near his feet pull back through the soil as his spine arches, bones grinding, his whole frame swelling larger, heavier, wrong in a way no living body should move.

For a brief moment, he looks up — and there is still something human in his eyes.

Then his jaw stretches, teeth forcing longer as a deep roar tears out of him, loud enough to shake the silence from the trees.

**Save:** DC 13 Constitution save.
**Damage:** 6 (3 if saved)

A massive bear now stands where the druid had been, breathing hard, staff still clutched awkwardly for one impossible second before it drops naturally to all fours.

It hesitates.

Shaking.

As though fighting itself.

Then it turns and crashes into the forest at full speed, tearing through branches and undergrowth until the sound vanishes deeper into the trees.

And just like that—

the clearing is silent again.
