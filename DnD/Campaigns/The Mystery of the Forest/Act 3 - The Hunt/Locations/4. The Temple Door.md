
## Overview 

**Scene Type**: Transition

**Setting**: Wilderness

**Encounter Type**: Puzzle

**Opening Description:** 

The party enters the courtyard. A massive stone door, carved with twisting vines and glowing runes, stands before them. A single button pulses faintly at its center.

“30…” the number flashes above the button. The air grows thick. Shadows stretch and twist. Roots coil and shift subtly. You feel the weight of the temple’s gaze. You look around for ways to enter you see glowing runes, a pulsing cocoon, a shrine that bubbles ominously, levers waiting to be pulled, and vines whispering familiar words.

## Actions

Timer 0. The countdown stops. With a deep rumble, the roots curl back, and the stone door slowly swings open. Bioluminescent moss bathes the passage ahead in gentle light. The temple exhales, satisfied. The grove is silent again — but the echoes of Bernard’s presence feel closer than ever.
