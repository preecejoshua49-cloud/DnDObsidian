## Setup 

### **Initial Tracking (5–7 min)**

- **Description:** The forest floor is churned, moss flattened, and branches broken as if a massive animal passed. The air smells damp, slightly metallic. Roots occasionally twitch underfoot.
- **Skill Opportunities:**
    - **Perception DC 12:** Detect faint prints — paw-like and humanoid, alternating.
    - **Survival DC 13:** Identify the most recent path through undergrowth.
    - **Nature DC 14:** Plants nearest trail appear unnaturally healthy.
    - **Investigation DC 12:** Torn patch of fabric snagged on root, matching cloak colour.

## Challenge 

As you step into the clearing, the air shifts. The shadows of the trees seem to ripple, and before you can react, a figure peels away from the bark itself — a dryad, her hair like leaves, eyes glinting with something ancient and wild. A low, menacing growl rolls through the underbrush… and then another. From either side of the clearing, the sound of wolves’ howls and snapping twigs crescendos, closing in, circling you like predators aware you’ve entered their domain.
